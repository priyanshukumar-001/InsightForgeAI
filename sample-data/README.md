# Sample Datasets for InsightForge AI

These sample datasets are provided for easy demonstration, testing, and exploration:

1. **`sales_sample.csv`**:
   - 25 rows, 10 columns
   - Retail sales transactions with categories (Electronics, Home Appliances, Office Supplies), dates, regions, unit prices, discounts, and payment methods.
   - Ideal for testing: revenue KPIs, sales by category bar charts, discount vs total scatter plots, time trends.

2. **`customer_churn_sample.csv`**:
   - 20 rows, 10 columns
   - Telecom subscriber data with contract types, tenure, monthly charges, and churn flags.
   - Ideal for testing: churn rate calculation, correlation between tenure and churn, box plots of monthly charges.

3. **`employee_performance.csv`**:
   - 15 rows, 9 columns
   - HR analytics data with departments, salary, experience, projects completed, and performance ratings.
   - Ideal for testing: department comparison, salary vs experience correlations, top performers.

*Note: In the application UI, these datasets can be preloaded with a single click and are always clearly labelled as "Sample Data".*
